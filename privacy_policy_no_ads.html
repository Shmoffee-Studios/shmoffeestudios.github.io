ShmoffeeStudios - Privacy Policy (games without ads)

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy applies to the games and apps published by ShmoffeeStudios ("we", "us") on Google Play and the Apple App Store that do not show ads (each a "game"). It explains what information is involved when you play, who handles it, and the choices you have. ShmoffeeStudios is responsible for this information (the "data controller"). Games that show ads are covered by our privacy policy for games with ads.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, or account, and we do not run our own servers that store your personal information.
  • These games show no ads and contain no advertising or analytics software. They do not access your advertising ID.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device (and in your device's own Google backup, if you have it turned on).
  • Some games have optional online leaderboards through Google Play Games, which use your Play Games profile name and your scores.
  • We do not sell or share your information.

Information stored on your device

Our games save your progress and settings in the game's private storage on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game, or when you use an in-game option to erase save data where one is available.

Some games also save a random identifier created by the game itself, used only to keep game statistics correct if your progress is combined from several devices. It is not linked to you or your account, and it is never sent to us.

Android backup: if Android Backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up a game's progress to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game or move to a new device. This backup is made and stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your device's settings.

Some games let you share a picture or result. That picture is created on your device and only goes where you choose to send or save it. We do not upload it anywhere.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

Some games offer global leaderboards through Google Play Games Services (provided by Google LLC). If you have a Play Games profile, the game signs in to it automatically and submits your scores. Your Play Games profile name, avatar and scores may then be visible to other players on the leaderboards, according to your Play Games settings. Google may also collect information about your use of Play Games under the Google Privacy Policy and the Play Games privacy settings.

We do not receive your email address or contact details through Play Games. You can change who sees your profile, hide your activity, or delete your Play Games profile in the Play Games app or in your Google account settings. Without a Play Games profile, the games work normally and your scores stay on your device.

Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

  • Performance of a contract: saving your progress and settings so the game works as you expect.
  • Legitimate interests: submitting scores to leaderboards when you use Play Games, which you control through your Play Games settings.

Google may transfer and process information outside your country, including in the United States, using legal safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Your choices

  • Leaderboards: manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Play Games app or your Google account.
  • Backup: turn Android Backup on or off in your device's settings.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game. Save data on your device is deleted with it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA and other U.S. state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we do not hold personal information that identifies you, most requests are best handled directly through the settings above or with Google, which holds the Play Games and backup data. You can also contact us and we will help.

U.S. residents (including California): we do not sell or share your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Purchases

If a game offers in-app purchases, payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only for leaderboards, in games that have them.
  • Vibrate: used for haptic feedback in some games. You can usually turn haptics off in the game's settings.

Our games do not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos (beyond saving a shared image when you ask to).

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. These games contain no ads and send no personal information to us. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and security

We do not store your personal information on our own servers. Save data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game. Information collected by Google (for Play Games and Android backup) is kept according to Google's retention policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date. If the changes are significant, we will also mention them in the game's update notes.
