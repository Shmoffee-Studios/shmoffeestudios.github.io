ShmoffeeStudios - Privacy Policy (games with ads)

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy applies to the games and apps published by ShmoffeeStudios ("we", "us") on Google Play and the Apple App Store that show ads (each a "game"). It explains what information is collected when you play, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. ShmoffeeStudios is responsible for this information (the "data controller"). Games without ads are covered by our privacy policy for games without ads.

The short version

  • We do not ask for your name, email, or account, and we do not run our own servers that store your personal information.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device (and in your device's own Google backup, if you have it turned on).
  • Some games have optional online leaderboards through Google Play Games, which use your Play Games profile name and your scores.
  • These games show ads. Our advertising partner, Google AdMob, collects device information such as your device's advertising ID to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. You can limit this at any time (see "Your choices").
  • We do not sell your information for money.

Information stored on your device

Our games save your progress and settings in the game's private storage on your device. This data is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game, or when you use an in-game option to erase save data where one is available.

Some games also save a random identifier created by the game itself, used only to keep game statistics correct if your progress is combined from several devices. It is not linked to you, your account or your device's advertising ID, and it is never sent to us.

Android backup: if Android Backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up a game's progress to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the game or move to a new device. This backup is made and stored by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your device's settings.

Some games let you share a picture or result. That picture is created on your device and only goes where you choose to send or save it. We do not upload it anywhere.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

Some games offer global leaderboards through Google Play Games Services (provided by Google LLC). If you have a Play Games profile, the game signs in to it automatically and submits your scores. Your Play Games profile name, avatar and scores may then be visible to other players on the leaderboards, according to your Play Games settings. Google may also collect information about your use of Play Games under the Google Privacy Policy and the Play Games privacy settings.

We do not receive your email address or contact details through Play Games. You can change who sees your profile, hide your activity, or delete your Play Games profile in the Play Games app or in your Google account settings. Without a Play Games profile, the games work normally and your scores stay on your device.

Advertising

Games that show ads use Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC). When an ad is requested or shown, the AdMob software inside the game collects and sends the following to Google:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID on Android, IDFA on iOS if you allow tracking) and app-specific identifiers.
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it (country or city level, not precise GPS location).
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, network type, app version.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, tapped, or watched, and diagnostic data such as ad load errors or crashes in the ad software.

This information is used to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have allowed it, show ads that are personalized to your interests. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your choices, your region, and your device settings. Non-personalized ads still use some device data (for example, to count ad views and prevent fraud), but are not based on your past behavior.

Google processes this information under its own privacy policy. To learn more, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google AdMob and user privacy

We do not receive your advertising ID or other personal information from Google. We only see aggregated reports (for example, total ad views and earnings).

Consent

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland: before personalized ads are shown, the game displays a consent message (provided through Google's consent management platform). You can accept, refuse, or choose specific purposes. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time from the game's privacy option (for example a PRIVACY button in the main menu or settings), where available, or by reinstalling the game.

iOS (iPhone and iPad): our games will ask your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not accessed, and any ads shown are not based on tracking you across other companies' apps and websites.

Legal bases (EEA and UK users)

  • Consent: for personalized advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device for advertising purposes.
  • Legitimate interests: for showing non-personalized ads, measuring ads, and preventing fraud, where permitted by law.

Google may transfer and process information outside your country, including in the United States, using legal safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Your choices

  • Android: go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device) to reset or delete your advertising ID. Once deleted, apps can no longer use it for personalized ads.
  • iOS: go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to allow or deny tracking for each app, or turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".
  • Google ad settings: manage ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game. Save data on your device is deleted with it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA and other U.S. state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

Because we do not hold personal information that identifies you, most requests are best handled directly through the settings above or with Google, which holds the advertising data. You can also contact us and we will help.

U.S. residents (including California): sharing device identifiers with ad partners for personalized ("cross-context behavioral") advertising may be considered "selling" or "sharing" personal information under some state laws. We do not sell your information for money. To opt out of this sharing, use the game's privacy option where available, delete or reset your advertising ID (Android), deny tracking (iOS), or enable a Global Privacy Control signal where supported. We will not treat you differently for using your privacy rights.

Purchases

If a game offers in-app purchases, payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to load and show ads.
  • Advertising ID (Android): used by the ad software as described above.
  • Vibrate: used for haptic feedback in some games. You can usually turn haptics off in the game's settings.

Our games do not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos (beyond saving a shared image when you ask to).

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Where a game is directed at children, or where a player has identified as under the age of digital consent in their region, we configure ads to be served as child-directed and non-personalized, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Google Play Families Policy, and Apple's guidelines for apps for kids. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our games, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and security

We do not store your personal information on our own servers. Save data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the game. Information collected by Google (for ads, Play Games and Android backup) is kept according to Google's retention policy. Data sent by the ad software is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date. If the changes are significant, we will also mention them in the game's update notes.
