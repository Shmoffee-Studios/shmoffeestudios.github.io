Sprout: Pocket Terrarium - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Sprout: Pocket Terrarium ("Sprout", "the app") is made by ShmoffeeStudios. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

Sprout does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

What the app stores on your device

Sprout saves your garden (your plants, dew, jars, ornaments and settings) in a file in the app's private storage on your device. This file never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, or when you choose "Erase save data" in Settings.

Sharing a picture of your garden

If you tap the share button, Sprout makes a picture of your garden on your device. The picture only goes where you choose to send or save it. Sprout does not upload it anywhere.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: used for gentle haptic feedback. You can turn haptics off in Settings.

Sprout does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or the internet.

Children

Sprout does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
